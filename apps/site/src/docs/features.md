## Daily Brief

Every morning, Quillby scans your configured sources — RSS feeds, Reddit communities, Medium publications, niche blogs — and delivers a curated summary of what's relevant to your audience. Each story comes with a suggested angle, a hook, and key insights so you can decide in seconds whether to write about it.

**Just ask:** "What should I post today?"

## Content Cards

Each brief generates content cards. A card is more than a headline — it's a ready-to-use story idea with:

- A thesis — the core argument you could make
- Key insights from the source article, summarized
- Multiple angle options — different ways to approach the topic
- Hook suggestions — opening lines tailored to different platforms
- A relevance score — how well it matches your profile

**Curate your queue:** Shortlist what you want to write, skip what doesn't fit, and clear your list when you're done.

## Draft in Your Voice

When you pick a card, Quillby writes a full draft — not generic AI text, but a post that sounds like you. It draws from:

- Your workspace profile (role, industry, audience, platforms)
- Voice examples you've saved from previous drafts you approved
- The actual source article, so the post is grounded in real content

**Supported formats:** LinkedIn posts, Twitter threads, blog articles, newsletters, or any custom format you describe.

**Just say:** "Write a LinkedIn post from card 3" or "Turn this into a newsletter draft."

## Workspaces

Keep your projects separate. Each workspace has its own:

- Profile and audience definition
- Content sources and feeds
- Voice examples and style preferences
- Drafts and published posts
- Campaigns and content plans

**Use cases:** One workspace per client (if you're a freelancer), per brand (if you run multiple), per newsletter, or per content category.

**Just say:** "Create a workspace for my B2B SaaS client" or "Switch to my newsletter workspace."

## Voice Memory

Quillby learns your style from the drafts you save. The more you use it, the less you edit. Memory types include:

- **Voice examples** — full posts that define your tone and rhythm
- **Style preferences** — things like paragraph length, formatting, sentence structure
- **Content guidelines** — what to focus on and what to avoid
- **Audience insights** — what your readers respond to

Each workspace has its own memory, so you can sound completely different for different clients.

## Campaigns

A single post is easy. Coordinating a month of content across topics and platforms takes planning. Quillby's campaign system lets you:

- Design multi-stage campaigns with automatic handoffs between stages
- Save successful campaign structures as templates
- Track progress as Quillby advances through each stage automatically

**Just say:** "Create a product launch campaign" or "Start my weekly LinkedIn series campaign."

## Content Planning

Stay on top of your publishing schedule with built-in planning tools:

- **Task board** — see what's queued, in progress, and completed
- **Content calendar** — a weekly view of upcoming posts and deadlines
- **Today queue** — what needs your attention right now

All inside Claude, no separate app or tab needed.

## Media Generation

Quillby can generate images and audio to accompany your posts. This is optional and requires connecting a supported service.

**Images:** Generate feature images, social cards, and illustrations using OpenAI or Replicate.
**Audio:** Turn posts into podcast-style audio using ElevenLabs or MiniMax.
**Voice cloning:** Create a voice clone from a reference recording (ElevenLabs).

**Just say:** "Generate an image for this post" or "Turn this into a podcast snippet."

## Sessions

When you're working on a specific campaign or task, you can start a focused session. Sessions help Quillby keep context and stay on track without mixing in unrelated content.
