## Step 1: Install Quillby

Follow the [installation guide](/docs/install) for your platform. After installing, restart Claude Desktop.

## Step 2: Set up your workspace

In Claude, say:

> Set me up with Quillby

Claude will ask you a few questions:
- What do you do? (e.g., "I'm a marketing consultant for B2B SaaS companies")
- Who is your audience? (e.g., "CTOs and VP-level engineering leaders")
- What platforms do you post on? (e.g., "LinkedIn and my newsletter")

Answer naturally. This creates your first workspace and saves your profile.

## Step 3: Discover your sources

Quillby automatically finds relevant RSS feeds, Reddit communities, and Medium articles for your topics. You can also add specific feeds manually:

> Add the RSS feed for TechCrunch to my workspace

## Step 4: Get your daily brief

Every morning, ask:

> What should I post today?

Quillby reads your feeds, ranks what's relevant to your audience, and presents a set of content cards — each with a thesis, angle, and hook.

## Step 5: Write a post

Pick a card and ask:

> Write a LinkedIn post from idea 3

Quillby drafts a publish-ready post in your voice. Review it, ask for adjustments, and save it:

> Save this draft

## Working with multiple projects

Quillby supports multiple workspaces — one per client, brand, newsletter, or campaign:

> Create a workspace for my B2B SaaS brand

Each workspace has its own profile, memory, feeds, and drafts. Content never mixes between workspaces.

> Switch to my newsletter workspace

## Teaching Quillby your voice

The more examples Quillby has, the more accurately it writes like you. When Claude writes a post you're happy with, say:

> Add this post to my Quillby voice examples

Quillby saves it inside the current workspace. Every future post draws on those examples.

To check what Quillby knows about your style:

> What do you know about my writing style?
