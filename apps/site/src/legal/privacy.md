title: Privacy Policy
updated_at: "2026-06-04"
---

Quillby runs locally on your machine. No data is sent to external servers unless you explicitly configure a provider (e.g., Replicate for image generation).

## Data Storage
- Workspace data is stored at `~/.quillby/` on your local filesystem.
- Quillby does not collect telemetry, analytics, or usage data.
- No account is required. No data leaves your machine unless you configure a provider API key.

## Provider APIs
When you configure a provider (ElevenLabs, Replicate, etc.), your API key is stored in your system keychain or an encrypted file. Data sent to these providers is subject to their respective privacy policies.
